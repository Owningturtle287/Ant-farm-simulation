GROW AT YOUR OWN PACE

Colony & reproduction

Egg laying and development are separate. Existing brood can still emerge when laying is off. Food, protein, care, moisture, and temperature continue to affect development.

At normal development, the model spans about 28 colony days under ideal conditions. Each colony day is compressed into ten minutes at 1×. Use higher time speeds to observe longer periods.

Changing the ceiling never removes existing ants.
LAYERS OF THE CLEARING

Terrain guide

Loose mound soil

Fresh, coarse pellets with mineral fragments. Excavated earth has a bare surface, without a grass cap.

Organic topsoil

Dark earth, roots, and organic fragments beneath undisturbed grass.

Sandy subsoil

Fine, pale grains. Relatively easy to excavate and carry.

Compact clay

Dense, horizontal seams. Digging takes longer, especially in poor moisture conditions.

Stone & deep mineral soil

Angular stone interrupts deeper layers. Ants must choose another direction around rock.
